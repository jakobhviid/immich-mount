Immich Mount is an independent, unofficial project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Immich project or FUTO. "Immich" is used only to describe the server this software works with, and remains the mark of its owner.

Immich Mount puts a self-hosted Immich library in Files, Dolphin, Finder and every file open and upload dialog, as a drive called Immich Mount. Attach your holiday photos to an email, open a video in your editor, or drop new pictures in to upload them, without downloading the library first.

At home on your desktop

The same drive everywhere, and on each platform an app that looks and works like the platform's own: no foreign toolkit and no theme of its own.

GNOME

A libadwaita app, the drive in Files' sidebar with its eject button, the credential in GNOME Keyring, and a tray icon where the AppIndicator extension is on.

KDE Plasma

A Kirigami app, the drive in Dolphin's Places with thumbnails from the server, the credential in KWallet, and a tray icon in Plasma's system tray.

macOS

A menu bar app, the library in Finder's sidebar through Apple's File Provider, as iCloud Drive's is, the credential in the Keychain, and updates signed by the project and notarized by Apple.

What it does

Your library as folders

The timeline by year and month, albums, people and places, named the way Immich names them.

Thumbnails from the server

Browsing a folder never downloads the photos in it.

Originals on demand

A file is fetched only when a program opens it, and only the parts it reads: a video seeks without downloading the whole file.

Uploads by copy and move

Drop photos and videos into Upload and they are uploaded unchanged; drop a folder and its folders can become albums. A move never loses a photo; every other folder is a read-only view.

Changes within seconds

What changes in Immich shows in the drive within seconds.

Native on your desktop

GTK and libadwaita on GNOME, Kirigami on KDE Plasma, a tray icon where the desktop has a tray, and on the Mac a menu bar app with Finder's own integration. In English and 20 more languages.

What it does not do

Sync or mirror your library: libraries of hundreds of gigabytes stay on the server. Edit, rename or delete what is already in Immich, or replace Immich's own apps: browsing photo by photo, search, faces, sharing and editing stay in Immich's web and mobile apps.

Install

You need an Immich server at v3.2 or later.

Linux for your system

With Homebrew, on any Linux that has it (Bazzite included, with nothing layered onto the system), the tap is trusted once, as Homebrew 6 and later ask, and one command then installs the app, the drive and the command line:

brew trust --tap jakobhviid/tap
brew install jakobhviid/tap/immich-mount
