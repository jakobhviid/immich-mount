Immich Mount

Your Immich photos and videos as a drive in your file manager.

Immich Mount puts a self-hosted Immich library in Files, Dolphin and every file open and upload dialog, as a drive called Immich. Attach your holiday photos to an email, open a video in your editor, or drop new pictures in to upload them, without downloading the library first.

What it does

Your library as folders

The timeline by year and month, albums, people and places, named the way Immich names them.

Thumbnails from the server

Browsing a folder never downloads the photos in it.

Originals on demand

A file is fetched only when a program opens it, and only the parts it reads: a video seeks without downloading the whole file.

Uploads by copy and move

Drop photos and videos into Upload and they are uploaded unchanged. A move never loses a photo; every other folder is a read-only view.

Changes within seconds

What changes in Immich shows in the drive within seconds.

Native on your desktop

One app, in GTK and libadwaita on GNOME and in Kirigami on KDE Plasma. Sign in with your server's single sign-on, a password or an API key.

What it does not do

Sync or mirror your library: libraries of hundreds of gigabytes stay on the server. Edit, rename or delete what is already in Immich, or replace Immich's own apps: browsing photo by photo, search, faces, sharing and editing stay in Immich's web and mobile apps.

Install

You need an Immich server at v3.2 or later.

Linux for your system

With Homebrew, on any Linux that has it (Bazzite included, with nothing layered onto the system), one command installs the app, the drive and the command line:

brew install jakobhviid/tap/immich-mount
